2. Deletion Vector in Databricks?
> DVs are storage optimizations in databricks
> Dvs allow for marking rows as deleted without actually rewriting the entire parquet file containting those rows.
> It is helpful when your performing delete, update, merge operations
> Dv is optimizing data write operation by Avoiding rewriting unchanged(same) data into another files.
